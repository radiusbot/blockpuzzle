Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Block Puzzle ("the app", "we") is a calm, offline puzzle game. We do not run any servers and we do not collect your personal information. The app does show ads from third-party ad networks, and this page explains plainly what they may collect and how you stay in control.

Summary

No account, no sign-in, no analytics SDK of our own.

Your game progress, scores and settings stay on your device.

Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use AppLovin as a mediation partner. These ad partners may collect device identifiers and related data as described below.

Payments for "Remove ads" are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your payment details.

Information stored only on your device

The app stores your current game, best score, game statistics, settings (sound, music, haptics, theme, accessibility options), whether you own "Remove ads", and a small count of ads shown (used to limit how often ads appear). This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is never sent to us.

If the app hits an unexpected error, a short technical log is written to the same private storage to help keep the game stable. It never leaves your device.

Advertising

The app shows a banner ad and, between games and never during play, an occasional full-screen video ad. If you choose to, you can also watch a reward video to earn an extra undo. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Through Google's mediation, AppLovin (AppLovin Corp.) may also compete to fill an ad.

To deliver, measure and, where you allow it, personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, these ad partners may collect: your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted), IP address, device and operating-system information, app information, interactions with ads, and approximate location derived from your IP address. This collection is done by the ad partners' software, under their own privacy policies:

Google: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

AppLovin: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

If you buy "Remove ads", the app stops showing banner and between-game ads. Optional reward videos (watched only if you choose to, for extra undos) stay available, so the AdMob SDK keeps one ready in the background.

Your choices

European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: after your first game, the app shows Google's consent form so you can choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy & ad choices.

iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission before your advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used for tracking. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android: you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings (Google → Ads, or Privacy → Ads).

If you decline personalisation, you may still see ads, but they will not be based on your activity in other apps.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their terms and privacy policies. We receive only a confirmation that the purchase was made, never your name, email or payment details. Purchases can be restored from Settings → Restore purchase.

Crash reports and statistics

Apple and Google may share anonymous crash reports and aggregated usage statistics with us if you have allowed this in your device settings (for example "Share with App Developers" on iOS). We use them only to fix problems and improve the game.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are restricted to content rated suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app's ad partners, contact us and we will help you reach them.

Retention and deletion

Data stored by the app lives only on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes it.

Data collected by ad partners is kept and deleted according to their own policies linked above. You can also reset your advertising identifier at any time, which disconnects previously collected ad data from your device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete or correct personal information, and to opt out of its "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. Because we do not hold personal information about you, requests about ad data are best made to the ad partners above; you can also opt out of targeted ads using the choices listed on this page. We do not sell personal information ourselves. Contact us and we will help with any request.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and the new version will be available in the app and at https://radiusbot.github.io/blockpuzzle/

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy can be sent to radiusbot@gmail.com.
